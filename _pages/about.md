Proud engineer by training, humbly turning into a neuroscientist. Currently a PhD candidate at EPFL, Switzerland.

When not neuroscienting, I enjoy running, reading, watching movies and spending time with the people I love.
You can talk to me about the brain, neuro-reductionism, AI (not too enthusiastically), geopolitics, the meaning of life, and which are the best thermal baths in Switzerland. 

My research so far has been focused on decision-making. More broadly, I am interested in understanding the neural basis of cognition, and I seek to do that by explicitly bridging the computational, algorithmic and implementation levels of brain function.
